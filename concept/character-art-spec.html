Character Art Spec — Custom Cosmic Direction

Replaces the abstract "Cut Light" polygons with entities that have real identity, while staying inside requirements §1.2.5 — cosmic and astronomical, deliberately secular. No demons, no undead, no mythological or religious forms. Every creature here is a structure the Cycle produced: debris, lenses, masses, fractures.

Each card carries the vector concept, the in-game stats it must match, and a generation prompt for producing a painted version later. The vector art is production-usable as-is.

Shared Style Anchor

Prefix every generation prompt with this block. Consistency across the roster comes from reusing it verbatim — changing it per entity is what makes a set look mismatched.

top-down 2D game sprite, flat vector illustration, faceted geometric forms, strong single-source rim lighting, cosmic / astronomy science-fiction, strictly secular — no religious, occult, demonic or mythological symbolism, palette limited to deep indigo #0d0a1a, plate violet #2a2340, and one accent hue, clean readable silhouette at 64x64, centered, transparent background, no text, no UI

Player & Enemy Roster

The Seeker

Player

A diver in sealed plate armour, cut in even facets — the only deliberate, symmetrical form in the game. Everything else is debris; the Seeker is made. The visor and chest core re-tint to the live Cosmic Cycle phase, so the player reads their own timing state off their own body.

#4dd0ff radius 15 rim = live phase

Mote

Enemy — Melee

The Cycle's smallest debris, and the first thing that comes for you. A bare arrowhead with two lit slits for eyes — no armour, no restraint, pure heading. Reads instantly as "fast and disposable" because there is nothing else to it.

#ff5c5c radius 12 hp x1.0 spd x1.0
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + a small aggressive arrowhead-shaped shard creature, sharp swept-back angular wings, two narrow glowing eye slits, crimson #ff5c5c rim light on dark maroon plating, no limbs, built entirely from straight cuts, fast and weightless silhouette

Caster

Enemy — Ranged

A suspended lens that never closes distance — it holds at range and fires focused light. The orbiting mote is the tell: when it completes a pass, a shot is coming. Kites at 175px, so its silhouette must stay legible small and far from the player.

#ffa63c radius 10 hp x0.6 range 175
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + a floating elongated diamond-shaped sentry, a large glowing amber lens set at its centre like a single eye, a thin elliptical orbit ring with one bright mote travelling along it, amber #ffa63c rim light, hovering, no contact with the ground, precise and watchful

Monolith

Enemy — Tank

Compacted Cycle-matter that stopped moving long enough to set. Broad hexagonal mass, visible seam lines, almost no accent colour — it is the one enemy that reads as heavy rather than lit. Slow and punishing; the silhouette should make you want to walk around it.

#8a8a9c radius 19 hp x2.6 dmg x1.7
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + a massive slow hexagonal slab of compacted stone-metal, thick layered armour plates with deep visible seam lines, blunt and wide, desaturated steel grey #8a8a9c with a single pale core plate, minimal glow, enormous weight, immovable presence

Fissure

Enemy — Splitter

Already broken when it reaches you. The crack running corner-to-corner and the two separate cores are a promise the game keeps — kill it and you get two. Telegraphing the split in the art is the whole point; a player should never be surprised twice by the same enemy.

#c77dff radius 11 splits x2 spd x1.1
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + a crystalline creature with a single jagged fracture line splitting it corner to corner, two separate glowing cores on either side of the crack, violet #c77dff rim light, visibly unstable and about to come apart, the two halves already reading as distinct shapes

The Gatekeeper

Semi-Boss — Wave 3

A hollow ring construct built around an exposed core. The core is the entire fight: it burns the colour of one Cosmic Cycle phase, and only takes full damage while the live Cycle matches it. The dashed inner ring spins faster as its AoE slam charges.

core = weak phase telegraphed slam HP bar
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + a hollow octagonal ring construct, thick outer frame, a single large faceted gem core suspended and exposed at its centre, a dashed rotating inner ring, violet #c23cff core glow, guardian silhouette, imposing but clearly built rather than grown

Hoardbound

Sector Boss

A vault that grew armour. It opens shielded and untouchable — the three cyan anchors tethered to it are the lock, and cutting all three drops the shield. The body reads as something holding treasure shut, which is exactly what the Extract/Descend choice is about.

shielded on spawn 3 anchors #ffd23c
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + a heavy armoured vault-creature, hexagonal plated shell sealed shut with a horizontal seam and a single gold lock-core, enclosed in a translucent spherical energy shield, three small cyan anchor crystals tethered around it by thin light filaments, gold #ffd23c trim on deep violet plating, hoarding posture

Rift Warden

Mega Boss — Sector 3

The thing destabilising the Cycle. Largest silhouette in the game, and the only one carrying both boss languages at once: an exposed phase core (Gatekeeper) and gold anchor plating (Hoardbound). Below ~55% HP the outer ring breaks and the anchor puzzle begins.

phase core, then shield two-stage at 55% largest silhouette
Generation prompt
[STYLE ANCHOR] + an immense two-stage guardian construct, outer gold-trimmed hexagonal frame with a fractured rotating containment ring, a huge violet faceted phase core burning at its centre, gold and cyan anchor nodes at its extremities, violet #c23cff and gold #ffd23c, unstable, reality distorting around its edges, final-boss scale

Sector Backgrounds

Backgrounds must never compete with entity silhouettes — they carry mood and depth only. Value stays below the darkest enemy plating so every creature reads as foreground.

The Fractured Approach

Sector 1

Still, cold, mostly empty. Thin cyan fracture lines drift across a near-black field — the Cycle is only just beginning to crack.

The Churning Depths

Sector 2

Everything is turning. Concentric amber orbits and curved motion trails sell acceleration — this is where the Cycle visibly speeds up.

The Convergence Core

Sector 3+

Unstable white centre bleeding violet. Broken dashed containment rings imply something is failing — the Rift Warden's home.

Production Notes

Anchor pointcentre (0.5, 0.5) — all existing collision code assumes a centred origin
Phase re-tintkeep rim/core on a separate layer so Phaser setTint() hits only that layer
Hit feedbackwhite flash overlay, 80ms — do not bake damage states into the sprite

Licensing

Every asset described here is original to this project — no third-party pack, no attribution obligation, no redistribution clause to track at Steam or Play Store submission. That was the deciding argument for generating rather than sourcing: the free packs that met the quality bar did not match the cosmic brief, and the ones that matched the brief carried licensing worth avoiding on a commercial release.
